Vocabook - AI-Powered Personal Reader & Narrator
Vocabook is a premium, privacy-first Android application that transforms how you consume digital literature. It combines high-performance on-device Large Language Models (LLMs) for summarization, neural Text-to-Speech (TTS), and AI image generation to create an immersive, personalized reading experience.

🛡️ Privacy & Security
100% On-Device AI: Vocabook is built on a "Local First" philosophy. No book content, voice data, or AI prompts ever leave your device. All processing—from summarization to image generation—is performed locally using your device's CPU/GPU/NPU.

✨ Features
AI Book Summarization: Get instant, high-quality summaries of long chapters using Google's Gemma 3 2B and Alibaba's Qwen 2.5 .5Bmodels.
AI Character Portraits: Generate detailed physical descriptions and visual portraits of characters using Stable Diffusion, optimized for mobile hardware.
Neural TTS: Professional-grade narration powered by Kokoro-82M, offering natural-sounding voice synthesis that works completely offline.
Intelligent Text Cleaning: Advanced, non-invasive parsing that works globally on any website to extract clean text while removing ads and clutter.
Custom Environments: Personalize your reading experience with glassmorphism UI, custom backgrounds, and immersive themes.
Full Privacy Browser: Navigate book sources with a built-in browser that supports ad-blocking and respects your data.

On-Device AI Infrastructure
LLM Engine: LiteRT-LM (The official successor to MediaPipe GenAI).
Primary LLM: Gemma 3 2B (Google DeepMind). (https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-3n-E2B-it-litert-lm/tree/main) -gated_Model
Fallback LLM: Qwen 2.5 0.5B Instruct (Alibaba/LiteRT Community). (https://huggingface.co/litert-community/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct/tree/main?show_LFS=true)
Image Generation: Stable Diffusion v1.5 (Absolute Reality 1.5 checkpoint).
TTS Engine: Sherpa-ONNX with Kokoro-82M models (Hexgrad/Kokoro).
Open Source Libraries
UI Framework: Jetpack Compose & Material 3.
Networking: Jsoup & OkHttp (Apache 2.0).
Inference: ONNX Runtime (MIT).
Document Processing: Apache POI & PDFBox-Android (Apache 2.0).
